## Scope and interpretation

- The twenty-year window is 2007–2026; 2006 is excluded. 2026 enters primary matched-YTD fits and each completed available month or event window.
- No full-calendar-year mean is assigned to unfinished 2026. Supporting full-year fits use eligible years only and report n separately.
- Completed monthly trends use their actual observation counts; unfinished months stay missing. Equal-decade seasonal differences require ten complete values in each group.
- Recent fast-track estimates are provisional and can change. A nominal-only YTD sensitivity reuses an earlier cutoff in all twenty years.
- All statistical intervals are conditional on the chosen model and do not propagate full measurement/calibration uncertainty. No acceleration, structural break, or future trajectory is inferred.
